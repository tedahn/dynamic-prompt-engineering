## Decision

State the decision in one falsifiable, operational sentence.

## Decision context

Describe the customer or business problem, constraints, urgency, and what happens if no decision is made.

## Evidence and assumptions

Link the relevant claim, source, customer-evidence, metric, experiment, and forecast IDs. Separate facts from assumptions and unresolved gaps.

## Options considered

For each viable option, record expected value, downside, reversibility, cost, timing, and evidence quality.

## Rationale and dissent

Explain why this option won. Preserve the strongest counterargument and conditions under which it would have won.

## Execution and verification

Name the owner, first action, success measure, guardrails, rollout, observability, rollback, and resolution date.

## Outcome

Complete after resolution: observed result, forecast error, surprises, follow-up, and whether the decision should become precedent.
