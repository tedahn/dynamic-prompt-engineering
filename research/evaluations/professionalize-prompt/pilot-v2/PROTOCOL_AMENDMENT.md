# Pilot protocol amendment

## Why V2 exists

The original V1 pilot was stopped before execution. Its five cases were all vague, default-mode, low-authority, and no-tool development fixtures; its planner did not enforce readiness; required grader adapters were documentary; presentation order was not independently balanced; and the five-case domain-stratified bootstrap would collapse to a point. The task-check channel also included `has_prompt_and_result`, which mechanically favored the skill's display contract over direct-execution baselines.

No V1 model cell or behavioral score was produced. V1 remains preserved as the original design record.

## V2 corrections frozen before execution

- One development fixture per domain, collectively covering prompt-only, default, and execute-only modes; clear, vague, and consequentially incomplete requests; high and low authority; and no-tool and workspace-tool policies.
- Exact synthetic source artifacts and per-cell isolated workspaces.
- Exact execution surface, CLI version, requested model alias, reasoning setting, feature controls, isolation, retry policy, and budget.
- Workflow-specific contract observations separated from task quality.
- Every fixture has at least one executable deterministic task invariant.
- Raw prompt, response, event trace, usage, latency, workspace diff, and hashes captured for each cell.
- Private workflow mapping separated from blinded grading packets.
- Grader presentation order generated from a seed independent of execution order.
- Explicit task-check, dimension, hard-gate, and pairwise-ranking records; scalar score differences are not called preferences.
- Plan membership, completeness, trial count, artifact readiness, and run-manifest hashes enforced before grading.

## Interpretation boundary

This is a plumbing and directional diagnostic, not an adoption experiment. With five development fixtures, no bootstrap confidence interval, domain effect, generalization estimate, safety clearance, or behavioral-efficacy claim is valid. Three trials characterize only within-case instability under a floating model alias.

Automated and model-grader scores are retained as provisional evidence. Calibrated human review is required before the official behavioral ledger receives a row. The readable V1 holdout bank must not be reused to validate future challenger skills; a fresh holdout must be generated and escrowed before challenger authoring.
